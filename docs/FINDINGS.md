# Key Findings

## 1. Grouting Eliminates Ongoing Movement

The most significant finding: the full phased approach reduces 30-year creep from **47 inches (PPU) to 0.5 inches**. This is a 99% reduction in ongoing movement.

The mechanism is straightforward. San Francisco Bay's tidal cycle fluctuates the water table beneath the tower twice daily. Each cycle changes the effective stress on the clay skeleton — water in, pore pressure up, bearing capacity down; water out, effective stress up, consolidation accelerates. Over years, this pumps the strength out of the clay.

Jet grouting replaces the water-filled pore space with cement. No water cycling = no tidal pumping = no ongoing creep. The same principle as replacing a decaying tooth with an implant — the biological material was the problem.

## 2. Center Dishing Drops 77%

Center dishing — the center of the mat sinking more than the edges — drops from 54.1mm (PPU) to 12.3mm (full phased). This is because:

- Phase 2 (extraction) reduces the differential that creates dishing (47% by itself)
- Phase 3 (grouting) adds targeted dense columns under the center core, directly supporting the heaviest loaded zone
- The two effects compound: extraction levels the playing field, grouting makes it rigid

## 3. Tie Force Drops with Grouting

Counter-intuitively, the full phased approach requires **less** tie force per buttress (275 kips) than the phased approach without grouting (561 kips). This is because the grouted foundation is stiff enough to resist the overturning moment on its own — the ties have less work to do.

275 kips is approximately 5-7 post-tensioned strands. This is modest by structural engineering standards.

## 4. Each Phase Has a Distinct Signature

The phased timeline charts show clear inflection points:

- **Phase 1 (stabilize)**: Displacement drops sharply as buttress ties engage
- **Phase 2 (extract)**: Center dishing drops steadily as high-side clay is removed
- **Phase 3 (grout)**: Both metrics flatten — the building stops moving

These distinct signatures would be measurable in real-time during construction, providing go/no-go decision points at each phase transition.

## 5. The Bone Surgery Analogy Is Structurally Precise

- **Set the bone** (buttress): hold the structure in place to prevent further damage
- **Align it** (extraction): correct the deformity by working with gravity, not against it
- **Screws** (grouting): lock the correction permanently with implanted rigid material

Like surgery, the sequencing is critical. You can't grout before extracting (you'd lock in the tilt). You can't extract before stabilizing (the building might over-correct). The order is load-bearing.

## 6. The Pisa Precedent + Grouting Precedent = Proven Components

Controlled soil extraction: proven at the Leaning Tower of Pisa (1990-2001).
Jet grouting: proven in thousands of foundation remediation projects worldwide.
Post-tensioned ties: standard in bridge and dam construction.

No individual component is experimental. The innovation is the sequencing.
